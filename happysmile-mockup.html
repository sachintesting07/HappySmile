When the enquiry arrives

Rules that hold in both

  • The treatment comes from the form, not the assistant. It is confirmed in chat, and the questions adapt to it.
  • The assistant always says it is a virtual assistant.
  • Anything clinical (pain, swelling), pricing or discounts goes to a human, with the conversation attached.
  • A call is never placed at night unless the patient asks for it.
  • Slots are real and shown as a list. After booking, the patient can change the time or clinic, or cancel.
  • Menu stays available after every path: raise a new enquiry for any treatment, manage the appointment, or talk to someone. Nobody has to go back to the website form.
  • Every path ends in the front-desk queue (Screen 2).
